# Optional local motion engine

The public skill works as finishing guidance with the user's installed editing tools. The separately obtained `video-editor-bassam` archive is not included or relicensed: no redistribution license was present in the supplied archive. Renaming it locally does not establish publication rights.

If you already possess a copy you are entitled to use, `scripts/import_local_engine.py` can store it under the git-ignored `.local/engines/video-editor-salman` directory. It does not publish it or install it over the open-source skill. Review its instructions there before use and preserve attribution. Do not copy that directory into commits.

On handoff, provide the job brief, confirmed captions, EDL and FPS. Inspect its real rendering capabilities before relying on them. Avoid automatic speech-silence cutting on gameplay, forcing motion scenes every few seconds, cropping a game recording as a full-screen talking head, and unannounced frame-rate changes. Respect existing user color/audio preferences. Keep meaningful engine checks; document and address checks that cannot understand a split gameplay/facecam layout. Do not disguise skipped checks as passed.

Check licenses for separately installed renderers, fonts, stock assets and music. A repository's MIT license does not replace those licenses or grant rights to somebody else's video.
